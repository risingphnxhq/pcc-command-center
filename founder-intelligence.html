RLS · Readiness Level System

Enterprise readiness Unassessed

Enterprise impact tier and disposition are separate. No GREEN is inferred from office rank, urgency, registered work, or an unsupported percentage. Missing identity, authority, Canon, telemetry, or receipt holds the affected advancement.

RLS-1 · Routine

Bounded, low-consequence work.

RLS-2 · Material

Material system, workflow, release, or dependent-team impact.

RLS-3 · Enterprise

Cross-system, cross-office, production, security, or continuity impact.

RLS-4 · Sovereign

Founder authority or highest-consequence commitment.

GREEN · ADVANCE YELLOW · RECONCILE RED · HOLD

GREEN normally requires at least 99% measured readiness and recorded evidence. YELLOW isolates and corrects gaps; RED holds affected dependent advancement. Later GREEN preserves earlier failed-gate history.

No enterprise readiness assessment or independent verification receipt is connected.

Assessment requires tier, percentage and scoring basis, accountable leader, measurement and independent-verification owners, evidence, dependencies, blockers, isolated work, correction, authorization, receipt, decision time, and next gate. Material code, schema, permission, workload, or environment changes require reassessment.
